About

Chorus is a software company building automated manufacturing operations for physical production.

Manufacturing is one of the largest industries still coordinated through fragmented systems and manual work. Every hardware company depends on a complex network of suppliers, contract manufacturers, warehousing and storage facilities, logistics carriers, compliance partners, and internal operators to move products from design to delivery. Yet much of that work still happens the way it did decades ago: CAD files passed over email, BOMs in spreadsheets, supplier feedback in PDFs and chat threads, and critical decisions scattered across systems.

We run that work differently. Chorus turns CAD files, BOMs, supplier quotes, engineering feedback, purchase orders, production updates, quality reports, logistics data, emails, spreadsheets, and conversations into a shared system of record for physical production. Software coordinates the work across the entire lifecycle, from design and sourcing through manufacturing, quality, fulfillment, and delivery, keeping every team, supplier, revision, decision, and handoff connected.

Every production cycle teaches the software something a great manufacturing operator knows: which issues will delay a build, which suppliers need follow-up, what needs approval, where production is slipping, and what has to happen next to keep a product moving. Each cycle makes the system better at coordinating the next. The companies that define the next era of manufacturing will operate this way. We intend to build the control plane they run on.
